# Launch operations

## Privacy requests

Log requests received at `hello@root.mx`, verify identity proportionately, acknowledge promptly, search the live database
and active processors, and answer within the applicable deadline. Record the decision without copying unnecessary request
content. Apply access, correction, export, restriction, objection, or deletion across processors and backups as applicable.

## Security incidents

Contain access, preserve relevant evidence, rotate affected credentials, identify data and people affected, document risk,
and notify Georgios Stagakis immediately. Assess GDPR supervisory-authority notification within 72 hours and applicable
U.S. state notification deadlines. Notify affected people when legally required. Record the decision and remediation.

## Content reports and appeals

Review reports promptly and objectively. Record the facts, applicable rule or law, decision, reasons, and whether automated
tools assisted. Email the reporter’s receipt and decision when an address was supplied. Treat replies requesting
reconsideration as appeals and conduct a fresh human review.

## Government requests

Verify the requester and legal authority, preserve the request, disclose only required data, and notify the affected person
unless prohibited. Seek counsel for unfamiliar, foreign, emergency, or overbroad demands.

## External launch actions

- Execute and retain DPAs and transfer terms for hosting, Resend, and OpenAI.
- Register the designated DMCA agent with the U.S. Copyright Office and renew the registration as required.
- Have qualified EU and U.S. counsel review the final terms, notices, retention periods, and service classification.
